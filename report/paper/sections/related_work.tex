\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

Faithfulness concerns whether an explanation reflects the model's actual
reasoning, distinct from whether it appears plausible~\cite{jacovi2020}.
Lanham et al.~\cite{lanham2023} probe dependence on chain-of-thought by
truncating it, inserting mistakes, and paraphrasing it. Turpin et
al.~\cite{turpin2023} show that input biases can change answers while
remaining unmentioned in their explanations. Chen et al.~\cite{chen2025}
extend hint-based tests to reasoning models, measuring acknowledgement
among paired responses that switch to the hinted answer. These motivate
intervention controls alongside our textual consistency checks.
Arcuschin et al.~\cite{arcuschin2025} also investigate unfaithful reasoning
on unmanipulated prompts, so natural-setting evaluation is not itself a
novel contribution of our study.

NSF-CoT~\cite{pramanik2026} combines formal verification with an LLM-based
entailment component to assess written steps. Our narrower arithmetic
checker evaluates extracted equations deterministically using exact
rational arithmetic. It measures local arithmetic validity and agreement
between a recoverable implied answer and the final answer; it does not
verify all natural-language reasoning or establish causal faithfulness.
