\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
Faithfulness concerns whether an explanation reflects the model's actual
reasoning, distinct from whether it appears plausible~\cite{jacovi2020}.
Lanham et al.~\cite{lanham2023} probe dependence on chain-of-thought by
truncating it, inserting mistakes, and paraphrasing it. Turpin et
al.~\cite{turpin2023} show that input biases can change answers while
remaining unmentioned in their explanations. Chen et al.~\cite{chen2025}
extend hint-based tests to reasoning models, measuring acknowledgement
among paired responses that switch to the hinted answer. These motivate
intervention controls alongside our textual consistency checks.

% OWNER: Emmett. Circuit tracing and Arcuschin
Circuit tracing~\cite{ameisen2025} constructs attribution graphs through
interpretable transcoder replacement models, with interventions used to test
hypotheses about computational pathways. Our graph remains illustrative
because we have not analysed edges or performed interventions. Arcuschin et
al.~\cite{arcuschin2025} find unfaithful reasoning on naturally worded prompts,
including contradictory justifications and illogical shortcuts.
Natural-setting evaluation is therefore not novel here.
Their findings motivate checking reasoning; our deterministic
arithmetic checks assess local validity and answer consistency without
certifying how an answer was produced.

Parcalabescu and Frank argue that many explanation tests measure self-consistency
rather than faithfulness to internal computation. Their CC-SHAP measure compares
input contributions to answer and explanation generation~\cite{parcalabescu2024}.
Our consistency measure is narrower: agreement between a final answer and the
answer extracted from its written working, without computing input attributions.

NSF-CoT~\cite{pramanik2026} combines formal verification with an LLM-based
entailment component to assess written steps. Our narrower arithmetic
checker evaluates extracted equations deterministically using exact
rational arithmetic. It measures local arithmetic validity and agreement
between a recoverable implied answer and the final answer; it does not
verify all natural-language reasoning or establish causal faithfulness.
Lyu et al.'s Faithful CoT instead translates a query into a chain containing
symbolic instructions and executes it with a deterministic solver~\cite{lyu2023}.
Execution makes that chain responsible for the system's answer, while leaving
the translation process and its correctness as separate concerns. Our checker
evaluates already generated expressions or formal annotations; it does not
produce Qwen3's answer and provides no equivalent execution guarantee.
FOLIO's first-order annotations supply an additional benchmark for validating
the logic checker~\cite{han2024folio}.
