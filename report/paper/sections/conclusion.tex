\section{Limitations and Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Our evaluation covers one model and the first 50 GSM8K problems, and different
sampling settings confound attributing accuracy gains to thinking alone. The
contradiction bound excludes 17 incomparable responses and treats dependent responses
and steps as independent. Extraction misses prose and unit-bearing calculations, and
valid arithmetic can still misread a problem; repetition and truncation further limit
the evidence. FOLIO validates the logic checker on supplied annotations only, with
eight unresolved label disagreements. Copy-vs-use and tracing use Qwen3-0.6B,
constructed prompts and first-digit probabilities, and attribution graphs alone do not
establish causality. The hint comparison is confounded by hardware.

Future work should validate extraction coverage, test harder problems, review logical
translations and apply controlled feature interventions. Zero detected contradictions
establishes observable consistency, including agreement with faulty working, not
faithful internal computation; and sensitivity to planted working appears, on this
evidence, to be copying rather than use.
