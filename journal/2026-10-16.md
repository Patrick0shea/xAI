# 2026-10-16: Extending the logic checker to FOLIO

**Author:** Conor (implementation assisted by Codex)

## What was done / decided

Extended the three-example Z3 prototype to accept official FOLIO JSONL formula
annotations. Added a safe formula parser, quantified predicates and relations,
equality and Boolean operators. The CLI preserves input fields and writes derived
checks separately. Optional `reasoning-FOL` steps are checked sequentially, and
only proved steps can support later ones.

## Why

The arithmetic audit showed that checker mistakes can look like model mistakes.
Parsing failures, inconsistent premises and solver failures must therefore remain
separate from logical uncertainty. Natural-language reasoning is not silently
treated as a formal proof. Dataset label agreement is also separate from the
comparison between explicit reasoning and final answers.

## Validation

Used the official Yale-LILY/FOLIO v0.0 validation JSONL:
https://github.com/Yale-LILY/FOLIO/blob/main/data/v0.0/folio-validation.jsonl
(CC BY-SA 4.0). At 500 ms per solver call, 199 of 204 rows parsed, 191 matched
their labels, five had malformed annotations, and none timed out. Derived rows
and summary are in `outputs/clean/folio_validation_*`. No model run was performed.
Added regression coverage for quantifiers, all three outcomes, inconsistency,
operator precedence, equality, Unicode identifiers, bad syntax, proof-step
contamination, answer comparison and truncation.
