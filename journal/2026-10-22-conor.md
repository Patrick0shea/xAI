## Why

The paper needed shorter explanations without losing the boundaries on its
claims. FOLIO validates the checker against supplied formulas; it is not a model
faithfulness experiment, so a separate results table is unnecessary. The short
Method C paragraph retains its denominator and failure cases.

The distinction between symbolic execution and checking a completed answer is
also essential. A solver-produced answer depends on its executable chain by
construction; agreement with a separately checked trace does not provide that
guarantee. The conclusion keeps this distinction alongside the model-size and
sampling limitations.
