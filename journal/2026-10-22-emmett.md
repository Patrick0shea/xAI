## Why

The earlier addition-only experiment cannot separate copying from arithmetic use
because both predict the same first digit. The extra multiplication in the
copy-vs-use control separates those predictions. Its results favour copying in
this prefix format, rather than completing the requested operation; first-token
behaviour still does not establish an internal mechanism or full-answer accuracy.

The model-size distinction also matters: tracing used a 0.6B transcoder
replacement model, while copy-vs-use used original 0.6B weights. Neither result
supports a claim about the 1.7B model's internal computation. Cutting repeated
explanation and merging tables keeps these qualifications visible while reducing
space. The tracing figure supplies an illustration, not additional causal evidence.
