## What was discussed / decided

Prepared the second two missing reading notes: Lyu et al. (2023) and Parcalabescu and Frank (2024), using their published ACL Anthology papers. Turpin and Chen remain outside this contribution. The notes include source locations, method summaries, limitations and recommendations for our paper.

## Why

The Related Work outline names both papers, but there were no local notes explaining their relevance. The central distinction for our draft is between a solver that produces an answer from generated instructions and a checker that evaluates an answer after generation. Our checker cannot claim the execution guarantee of Lyu et al.'s system.

The second note supports keeping our claims about written-step validity and answer-working agreement separate from claims about internal reasoning. It also distinguishes our arithmetic consistency check from CC-SHAP's input-attribution comparison.
