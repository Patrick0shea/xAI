## What was discussed / decided

**1. FOLIO moves from "drop" to "checker validation".** The 2026-10-15 plan proposed
dropping FOLIO from the results. Conor's validation run on the official FOLIO v0.0
validation set (`journal/2026-10-16.md`, `outputs/clean/folio_validation_summary.json`)
parsed 199 of 204 annotated rows, and 191 of those 199 matched their dataset labels.
That shows the Z3 checker works on real first-order annotations, so it goes in the
Method as a validated checker. It is **not** a faithfulness result: no Qwen3 output
was checked, and label agreement only tests the checker and the annotations.

**2. Two final experiments, and why each one is needed.**
- **Copy-vs-use (Emmett).** Our planted-working result (the model follows a wrong
  working 94–99% of the time on 8 prompts, `journal/2026-10-07-circuit-tracing.md`)
  has an obvious objection: the model might just copy the last number in the working
  rather than *use* the reasoning. Prompts where the answer needs one more step after
  the working (following the working, copying it and the true path give different
  first digits) separate the two. The same prompts are short enough to trace on
  Qwen3-0.6B, which is the closest we can get to closing the 0.6B/1.7B gap between
  the traced model and the model that wrote our GSM8K outputs.
- **Hint test (Fionn).** A matching answer is *consistent* with the reasoning, not
  necessarily *faithful* to it (2026-10-15, point 2). Our checker can only measure
  consistency. A misleading hint in the prompt tests faithfulness directly: if the
  answer follows the hint while the thinking never mentions it, the reasoning is
  consistent but not faithful. This was assigned to Conor on the 15th and is now
  Fionn's.

## Why

- The FOLIO result is worth reporting, but only for what it shows. Calling label
  agreement "faithfulness" would be exactly the overclaim our Discussion criticises.
- Without copy-vs-use, the planted-working result can't distinguish "uses" from
  "copies". Without the hint test, our only main result is a consistency null, which
  we have already argued can't establish faithfulness.
- A hard freeze is the only way to leave Thursday for cutting to the page limit and
  checking citations.
- The 74/78/83 history matters because the 74 appears in a committed journal entry
  and an earlier version reached the talk. Both stay as written (no backfilling), and
  this entry records the correction.
